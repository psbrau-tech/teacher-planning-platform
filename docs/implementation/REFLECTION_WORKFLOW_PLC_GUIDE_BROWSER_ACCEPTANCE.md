# Reflection workflow / PLC meeting guide browser acceptance

Use after governed deployment of this release.

## Teacher Friday closeout

- Confirm no floating Reflection Insights button appears on Dashboard, Course Setup, Weekly Plan, Administration, or Help.
- Complete/reopen a Friday closeout through Completed Weekly Packet review.
- Confirm the closeout stepper shows Step 4 Reflection insights and Step 5 Continue.
- Confirm Step 4 is marked optional and the teacher can continue without generating a recap.
- Open Reflection Insights from Step 4 and confirm the private recap still requires the no-student-data boundary confirmation.
- Generate a recap and confirm only the teacher's private submitted-reflection synthesis is shown.

## Administrator PLC workflow

- Open Administration reporting and generate the PLC Meeting Guide.
- Confirm the guide visibly contains a School Reflection Summary with common successes, common challenges, emerging themes, discussion questions, possible actions, and support needs when returned by the governed summary.
- Confirm the suggested meeting focus, aggregate formative-assessment planning snapshot when available, 40-minute protocol, and team action workspace remain present.
- Print the PLC Meeting Guide and confirm there are no blank/duplicate pages and that the summary remains readable.

## Boundary checks

- No student-specific information is introduced.
- No teacher ranking, quality score, effort/productivity score, or personnel-evaluation language is introduced.
- Team action workspace entries remain non-persistent.
