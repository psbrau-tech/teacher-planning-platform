# Administration consolidation / inline Reflection Insights browser acceptance

Use after governed deployment of this release.

## Administrator submission reporting

- Open Administration and confirm there is no separate administrator Friday Submission Status report.
- In Weekly submissions, select a Monday-starting week and one or more teachers.
- Confirm each teacher/course row shows Upcoming lesson plan and Completed weekly packet separately.
- Confirm submitted records use the green status treatment and outstanding records use the yellow needs-follow-up treatment.
- Confirm single-record and selected-record PDF review/download still work.

## Administrator instructional-learning flow

- Scroll through Planned daily formative-assessment mix and Week-over-week planned assessment trend.
- Confirm the next professional-learning section is **School Reflection Summary → PLC Meeting Guide** rather than a separate submission-status report.
- Generate the School Reflection Summary and confirm Common successes, Common challenges, Emerging themes, Discussion questions, Possible actions, and Support needs are visibly displayed when available.
- Confirm the PLC Meeting Guide follows the visible summary and uses it as the meeting evidence base.
- Print/preview the PLC Meeting Guide and confirm the same School Reflection Summary is included in the printed guide with the suggested focus, assessment snapshot when available, 40-minute protocol, and non-persistent action workspace.
- Confirm print pagination remains clean with no blank or duplicated pages.

## Teacher Friday closeout

- Confirm Reflection Insights remains optional Step 4 and Continue remains Step 5.
- Confirm no right-side Reflection Insights popup appears.
- Confirm the no-student-data confirmation, period controls, Generate private recap action, and generated private recap all render inline inside Step 4.
- Confirm the teacher can continue to Step 5 without generating a recap.

## Shared UI behavior

- Trigger a normal success notification such as Completed Weekly Packet ready for review and confirm it disappears automatically after approximately five seconds.
- Confirm the notification can still be dismissed manually before the timer expires.
- Open the Weekly Lesson Plan PDF, Completed Weekly Packet PDF, and Weekly Reflection PDF previews.
- Confirm the preview title remains on the left and Close preview remains consistently on the right across those views.

## Boundary checks

- No student-specific data is introduced.
- The School Reflection Summary remains anonymous/aggregate and non-evaluative.
- The private recap remains private to the teacher and non-evaluative.
- Scheduled teacher/admin email delivery remains inactive unless separately authorized and activated.
