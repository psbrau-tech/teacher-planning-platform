# Teacher Planning Platform — Privacy Policy

**Status:** Pre-Release Draft — Not Yet Effective  
**Effective date:** [TO BE APPROVED]

This Privacy Policy explains how Brau Consulting LLC ("Brau Consulting," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects information in connection with Teacher Planning Platform ("TPP"). TPP is intended for adult educators, administrators, and other authorized professional users.

## 1. TPP data boundary

TPP is designed for teacher, administrator, curriculum, standards, scheduling, lesson-planning, validation, reflection, export, professional-learning, reporting, notification, and related professional information. TPP is not designed to collect, store, or process student personally identifiable information or student education records.

Users must not submit student names, student identifiers, identifiable grades, individualized education program or Section 504 information, disciplinary information, student health information, identifiable student work, or other information that identifies or can reasonably be linked to a student.

If prohibited student information is submitted inadvertently, Brau Consulting may take reasonable steps to isolate, delete, or otherwise remediate the information consistent with security, legal, contractual, and technical requirements.

## 2. Information we collect

Depending on how an organization configures and uses TPP, we may collect:

### Account and identity information
- name;
- professional email address;
- organization, school, district, or employer affiliation;
- role and authorization information;
- authentication identifiers and account status.

### Professional planning content
- course and teaching-assignment information;
- curriculum materials and curriculum sequence information;
- instructional standards selections and source/version provenance;
- lesson plans, instructional-framework entries, schedules, planning exceptions, validation records, teacher-authored reflections, and related educator-created material;
- teacher decisions concerning AI suggestions, such as accept, edit, or reject actions;
- exports and information necessary to create them.

### Professional-learning and aggregate planning information
Where the applicable feature is enabled and authorized, TPP may derive professional-learning information from permitted professional content. Examples include:

- private AI-generated teacher recaps based on the teacher's own already-submitted professional reflections;
- anonymous/aggregate school PLC themes and related professional-learning discussion material derived from submitted professional reflections, subject to the applicable source-threshold and authorization controls;
- deterministic school-level counts of planned daily formative-assessment types already present in submitted lesson plans, such as exit tickets/slips or other checks for understanding; and
- transient PLC facilitation artifacts generated from an already-authorized aggregate school brief.

These features are not designed to collect student assessment results or student-level analytics. TPP's product governance prohibits representing these professional-learning/product signals as teacher-quality, ranking, effort, productivity, or personnel-performance scores.

### AI request and usage information
When a user invokes an approved AI-assisted feature, TPP may process the minimum professional context necessary to perform the request, such as selected standards, curriculum context, planning fields, or, for approved post-submission Reflection Intelligence, teacher-authored professional reflection context. TPP is designed not to include student-specific information in AI requests.

The required Weekly Reflection / PLC Discussion responses themselves are teacher-authored. TPP does not use generative AI to suggest, generate, complete, or rewrite those required responses. Reflection Intelligence, where enabled, analyzes already-submitted professional reflections after the teacher has authored and submitted them.

TPP may record operational metadata such as model/request usage, token or usage measurements, estimated cost, request status, error information, and teacher decision state where applicable. Secrets such as API keys must not be placed in application usage logs.

### Technical, product-usage, notification, and security information
We may collect information reasonably necessary to operate, secure, and improve TPP, including IP address, request timing, browser or device characteristics supplied by standard web requests, authentication events, application events, error information, security/audit records, and bounded product-usage events.

TPP may also record conservative active-interaction duration estimates for product-effectiveness measurement. These estimates use fixed 30-second activity-heartbeat event keys for product workflow areas such as Course Setup, Weekly Planning, Teacher Reflection, and Friday Closeout. TPP stops counting when the application tab is hidden or when recent interaction is absent. The active-time telemetry does not record keystroke contents, mouse coordinates, teacher-entered planning/reflection text, or continuous login duration. Product-duration reporting is restricted to the Platform Owner product-analysis role and is not provided through ordinary school/district administrator reporting.

Where professional email notifications are enabled, TPP may process the recipient's professional account email address and the minimized operational information necessary to create and deliver the approved notice. The first approved school-admin weekly digest is designed to carry school-scoped operational counts plus a link back to authenticated TPP rather than teacher reflection text, teacher-level exception lists, generated instructional insight, student information, or teacher-quality/performance content.

TPP may record bounded content-free notification-delivery metadata such as notification type, professional profile/school identifiers where required for governed delivery accounting, week/date, delivery state, and timestamps. The source-controlled scheduled-delivery design does not persist the recipient email address, email body, or Amazon SES MessageId in its delivery ledger.

### Support and communications
If you contact Brau Consulting, we may collect your contact information and the contents of the communication.

TPP may also use a user's professional account email address to send service-related or operational notices that are enabled for the applicable organization/account. Automated operational delivery, where enabled, is governed separately from marketing communications and is intended to carry only the professional information approved for that notification type.

## 3. How we use information

We use information to:
- authenticate and authorize users;
- provide lesson-planning, scheduling, standards, validation, AI-assistance, professional-learning, reporting, notification, and export functionality;
- save and retrieve user and institutional planning content;
- generate authorized private/aggregate professional-learning summaries and deterministic planning analytics;
- provide approved professional operational email notices when notification delivery is enabled;
- maintain audit history and accountability controls;
- provide customer support;
- monitor reliability, troubleshoot errors, and protect the security of TPP;
- measure AI usage and operational cost;
- measure product adoption and workflow efficiency using bounded product-usage and active-interaction metadata;
- maintain and improve TPP without using customer content in a manner inconsistent with this Policy or an applicable customer agreement;
- comply with law, enforce agreements, and protect rights and safety.

TPP's professional-learning, assessment-planning, notification, and product-usage features are not designed to make automated employment decisions or produce teacher-performance ratings. Customer organizations remain responsible for their own lawful personnel and evaluation practices.

## 4. AI model training

TPP currently uses OpenAI's business/API services for approved generative-AI functions. Current provider terms and the production account configuration must be reverified before this Policy is published or materially relied upon in customer contracting.

Brau Consulting's production policy is not to opt TPP customer content into third-party model training without an explicit governance review, corresponding update to this Privacy Policy and customer-facing disclosures, and any consent or contractual change required by law or agreement.

This statement must be reverified against the production AI-provider account and current provider terms before publication and at material provider/configuration changes.

## 5. How we disclose information

We may disclose information to:

### Service providers and subprocessors
We use vendors for cloud infrastructure, database/authentication, AI processing, professional operational email delivery when enabled, and related technical services. Material current providers and staged/conditional provider functions are identified in the TPP Subprocessor List. They may process information only for the purposes associated with providing their services to Brau Consulting, subject to applicable agreements and law.

### Customer organizations
If your account is provided or managed by a school, district, employer, or other customer organization, authorized administrators may have access to account, planning, reporting, submission, professional-learning, and audit information according to role permissions and the applicable institutional agreement. Platform Owner-only product-duration analytics are not part of the ordinary school/district administrator reporting surface.

School-level Reflection Intelligence and planned formative-assessment analytics are intended for authorized professional learning/operations within the product's governance boundary. Their existence does not authorize student-level analytics or convert TPP product-adoption/professional-learning signals into teacher-performance scores.

### Legal and safety disclosures
We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law or valid legal process; enforce agreements; investigate fraud, abuse, or security incidents; or protect the rights, property, and safety of Brau Consulting, users, customer organizations, or others.

### Business transactions
If Brau Consulting is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, sale of assets, or similar transaction, information may be disclosed as reasonably necessary for that transaction subject to appropriate confidentiality and legal protections.

## 6. Sale and advertising

TPP is an educator productivity service. Brau Consulting does not sell TPP customer planning content or account information for monetary consideration. TPP is not designed to use customer planning content for targeted advertising or cross-context behavioral advertising.

If the product later introduces advertising or a data practice materially different from this statement, the policy and product governance must be reviewed and updated before that change is enabled.

## 7. Cookies and local storage

TPP may use cookies, browser storage, session mechanisms, or similar technologies that are necessary for authentication, security, application state, and core functionality. TPP's first-party active-time measurement uses temporary browser storage to coordinate a short-lived tab lease so that multiple simultaneously open TPP tabs do not double-count the same user's active interaction. That lease stores a temporary tab identifier and expiration time rather than teacher planning content.

Any additional non-essential analytics, third-party tracking, session replay, or advertising technology must be separately reviewed and added to this Policy before production use.

## 8. Retention and deletion

Brau Consulting retains account and professional planning information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide TPP, maintain legitimate security/audit records, comply with applicable agreements and law, and complete approved deletion procedures.

The controlled pilot currently configures AWS application logs with a 30-day retention period. This does not mean every category of TPP data has a 30-day retention period.

A final production retention schedule for active planning content, deleted-account content, database backups, audit history, AI operational metadata, product-usage telemetry, professional notification-delivery metadata, application logs, and institutional termination is a release requirement and will be documented in the Data Retention & Deletion Policy before public launch.

Transient PLC facilitation formatting does not, by itself, create a retained server-side PLC action-note store in the current source-controlled design. If TPP later persists PLC action items, named owners, theme-lifecycle decisions, or team notes, retention/access/disclosure rules must be reviewed before that feature is enabled.

When information is deleted from active systems, residual copies may remain temporarily in backups, disaster-recovery systems, security records, or legally required records until those systems cycle or the applicable retention obligation ends.

## 9. Security

Brau Consulting uses administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect TPP information. Current pilot controls include HTTPS/TLS for deployed web traffic, role-based authorization, protected secrets, isolated cloud infrastructure, read-only container root filesystem configuration, database access controls, and bounded logging.

Source-controlled notification architecture is designed to keep elevated Supabase service-role credentials out of browser code and the interactive web task. Any scheduled professional-email worker that is later activated must use a separately governed execution path and least-privilege credentials.

No security system is perfect, and Brau Consulting cannot guarantee that information will never be accessed, used, or disclosed without authorization.

Additional details are provided in the Security & Data Practices document.

## 10. Security incident notification

Brau Consulting maintains an incident-response process for suspected security events. Notifications following a confirmed breach will be made as required by applicable law and customer agreements. Alabama's Data Breach Notification Act includes requirements concerning reasonable security measures, investigation, notification, and disposal of certain sensitive personally identifying information. The applicability and required notice for a particular event depends on the facts and affected information.

## 11. Individual and institutional requests

Subject to applicable law, contract, and organizational administration, users may request access to, correction of, or deletion of account information by contacting the privacy/support contact below. For institution-managed accounts, Brau Consulting may route the request through the authorized customer administrator where appropriate.

Deletion requests do not override records that Brau Consulting is required or permitted to retain for security, legal, contractual, backup, or dispute-resolution purposes.

## 12. Children and students

TPP is not intended for children or students. Brau Consulting does not knowingly offer TPP accounts to children for student use. The service's professional-user boundary does not authorize adult users to submit student data.

If Brau Consulting learns that TPP has received student information contrary to the service boundary, it will follow its incident/data-remediation procedures as appropriate.

## 13. U.S. service and international use

TPP is initially operated as a U.S.-focused service. The controlled pilot application infrastructure is deployed in the AWS `us-east-2` Region, while other service providers may process information in locations permitted by their agreements and configuration. Brau Consulting will review cross-border requirements before intentionally offering TPP in jurisdictions that require additional privacy terms or transfer mechanisms.

## 14. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when TPP, law, vendors, or our practices change. Material changes will be identified by an updated effective date and communicated when required by law or contract.
