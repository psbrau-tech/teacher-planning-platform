# Teacher Planning Platform — Accessibility Statement

**Provider:** Brau Consulting LLC  
**Status:** Pre-Release Draft — Not Yet Effective

Brau Consulting LLC is committed to making Teacher Planning Platform (TPP) usable by educators and administrators with disabilities.

## Our approach

TPP should be designed and maintained with accessibility considered throughout product development rather than treated only as a post-release remediation task. Relevant development practices include semantic structure, keyboard-operable controls, visible focus states, meaningful labels, sufficient contrast, understandable error messaging, text alternatives for meaningful non-text content, and compatibility with common assistive technologies where reasonably achievable.

## Public-school accessibility baseline

Public schools and other state/local government entities are subject to Title II of the Americans with Disabilities Act. The U.S. Department of Justice's web and mobile accessibility rule uses **WCAG 2.1 Level AA** as the technical standard for covered public-entity web content and mobile applications. DOJ guidance also makes clear that a public entity generally cannot avoid this responsibility by obtaining the web content or application through a vendor or contractor.

As of August 8, 2026, DOJ's April 2026 interim final rule extends the rule's compliance dates to April 26, 2027 for public entities with a total population of 50,000 or more and April 26, 2028 for public entities with a total population below 50,000 or special district governments. Other Title II obligations concerning effective communication, reasonable modifications, and equal opportunity continue to apply before those dates.

TPP will therefore use **WCAG 2.1 Level AA as the minimum public-school release baseline** for covered functionality. Where practicable, TPP may also test against WCAG 2.2 Level AA as an additional forward-looking engineering target, but WCAG 2.2 testing does not replace explicit verification against the applicable WCAG 2.1 Level AA legal baseline.

This statement does not represent that an independent accessibility audit has already certified full conformance.

**Release requirement:** Core teacher and administrator workflows must be tested against the applicable WCAG 2.1 Level AA criteria, including keyboard operation, focus order, accessible naming, contrast, zoom/reflow, validation/error behavior, and representative screen-reader use before this statement is published as an effective accessibility claim.

## Documents and exports

TPP may generate or populate PDF and other planning documents. Accessibility of an exported document can depend on the source template, export technology, content entered by the educator, and downstream software. For public-entity customers, conventional electronic documents can also fall within the DOJ Title II web rule depending on how they are provided and used; exceptions are fact-specific.

Brau Consulting will document known limitations and will not describe generated PDFs as fully accessible unless the specific export path has been tested for that claim. Accessibility of TPP-generated/exported documents used by a public school must be included in customer-specific accessibility review where applicable.

## Continuous improvement

Accessibility requirements apply to new features and material UI changes. Accessibility defects affecting core workflows should be tracked and prioritized as release-quality issues, particularly where TPP is provided to a public school or other Title II entity through a contractual or licensing arrangement.

## Review cadence

Review this statement and the application's accessibility evidence before public-school production use, before general release, after major redesigns, and at least annually thereafter.

## Primary legal source

U.S. Department of Justice ADA.gov guidance and rule materials for Title II web/mobile accessibility, including the current compliance-date extension, must be reverified before publication.
