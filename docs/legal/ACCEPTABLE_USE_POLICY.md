# Teacher Planning Platform — Acceptable Use Policy

**Provider:** Brau Consulting LLC  
**Status:** Pre-Release Draft — Not Yet Effective

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") applies to all use of Teacher Planning Platform ("TPP"). It is incorporated into the TPP Terms of Use and any institutional agreement unless that agreement expressly states otherwise.

## 1. Professional-use boundary

TPP is intended for adult educator, administrator, and related professional planning use. Users may use TPP for curriculum organization, standards work, scheduling, lesson planning, validation, reflection, reporting, export, and related professional functions made available by the service.

## 2. Student data is prohibited

Do not enter, upload, paste, import, transmit, or intentionally cause TPP to process student personally identifiable information or student education records.

Prohibited student information includes, without limitation:
- student names or initials when they identify a particular student;
- student identification numbers, usernames, email addresses, or contact details;
- identifiable grades, scores, attendance, discipline, behavior, or intervention records;
- IEP, Section 504, disability, accommodation, or special-education records;
- health, counseling, mental-health, medication, or medical information;
- demographic information tied to an identifiable student;
- identifiable student work, essays, assessments, recordings, photographs, or portfolios;
- parent/guardian information linked to a student;
- any other information that identifies or can reasonably be linked to a student.

When a planning task requires differentiation or discussion of learner needs, use generalized or de-identified instructional descriptions that cannot reasonably be linked back to a student.

## 3. Other sensitive data that must not be submitted

Unless Brau Consulting expressly approves a specific workflow in writing, do not submit:
- passwords, API keys, private cryptographic keys, authentication tokens, or other secrets;
- Social Security numbers, government identification numbers, bank or payment-card data;
- medical records or protected health information concerning any person;
- biometric identifiers;
- highly sensitive personnel records unrelated to the intended planning service;
- classified, export-controlled, or unlawfully obtained information.

## 4. Unlawful and harmful activity

You may not use TPP to:
- violate applicable law, regulation, court order, or contractual obligation;
- infringe intellectual-property, privacy, publicity, or other rights;
- threaten, harass, exploit, discriminate unlawfully against, or facilitate harm to another person;
- create or distribute malware, malicious code, credential theft, phishing, unauthorized surveillance, or other cyber abuse;
- gain unauthorized access to accounts, systems, data, or networks;
- interfere with TPP availability, bypass rate limits or security controls, or conduct load testing without authorization;
- misrepresent AI-generated material as an official source, legal requirement, or verified standard when it is not;
- use TPP to make automated high-impact decisions about identifiable students or other individuals.

## 5. Intellectual property and source materials

Submit only material you are authorized to use. You may use legitimate curriculum, standards, templates, and professional materials consistent with applicable licenses, fair-use principles where applicable, organizational policy, and law.

Do not use TPP to intentionally reproduce or distribute copyrighted material beyond your lawful rights.

## 6. AI safeguards

AI features are intended to assist professional judgment, not replace it. You may not intentionally defeat safeguards that require educator review, cause unapproved AI suggestions to overwrite governed planning content, or represent AI-generated standards interpretations as authoritative source text.

Do not prompt TPP to disclose system prompts, secrets, other users' information, protected internal configuration, or content you are not authorized to access.

## 7. Account security

Do not share individual account credentials. Promptly report suspected unauthorized access, lost credentials, or security vulnerabilities through the approved support/security channel.

## 8. Institutional rules

If your account is provided by an employer, school, district, or other organization, you must also comply with that organization's lawful technology, records, intellectual-property, and acceptable-use policies. Where an organizational policy is stricter than this AUP, the stricter organizational rule may apply to you.

## 9. Enforcement

Brau Consulting may investigate suspected violations and may restrict, suspend, or terminate access when reasonably necessary to protect TPP, users, customer organizations, or legal/contractual obligations. Where appropriate, Brau Consulting may preserve evidence or cooperate with lawful investigations.

For accidental submission of prohibited student or sensitive information, users should promptly notify Brau Consulting rather than attempting to conceal or repeatedly access the information.
