# Teacher Planning Platform — Institutional Services Agreement

**Provider:** Brau Consulting LLC  
**Status:** Pre-Release Draft — Not Yet Effective  
**Agreement date:** [ORDER FORM / SIGNATURE DATE]

This Institutional Services Agreement ("Agreement") is between Brau Consulting LLC ("Brau Consulting") and the school, school district, educational agency, employer, or other organization identified in the applicable order form or signature block ("Customer"). It governs Customer's institutional access to Teacher Planning Platform ("TPP").

## 1. Order of precedence

This Agreement, the applicable order form, the TPP Privacy Policy, Acceptable Use Policy, AI Use & Accuracy Notice, and any mutually executed addendum comprise the parties' agreement. If documents conflict, the following order applies unless the order form expressly states otherwise: (1) signed negotiated addendum; (2) signed order form; (3) this Agreement; (4) referenced policies.

## 2. Services

Brau Consulting will provide Customer access to the TPP services identified in the order form during the applicable term. TPP is an educator productivity and instructional-planning platform supporting curriculum organization, standards work, scheduling, lesson planning, validation, AI-assisted planning, reporting, and exports.

Specific pilot scope, implementation services, support levels, user quantities, templates, or integrations will be stated in the order form or implementation plan rather than implied by this Agreement.

## 3. Authorized users

Customer may authorize adult employees, contractors, or other professional personnel within the licensed scope to use TPP. Customer is responsible for identifying authorized users and administrators and for promptly notifying Brau Consulting when access should be changed or removed.

Each account must be used by the assigned individual. Customer administrators may have role-appropriate access to institutional planning, account, reporting, and audit information.

## 4. Data boundary — no student data

The parties agree that TPP is not designed or contracted to receive student personally identifiable information or student education records. Customer will not intentionally submit or direct users to submit such information.

Customer will instruct authorized users not to submit student names, identifiers, identifiable grades or assessment results, IEP/Section 504 information, disciplinary or health records, identifiable student work, or other student-specific records.

If either party discovers prohibited student data in TPP, it will notify the other party as appropriate and cooperate in reasonable containment, deletion, and remediation consistent with applicable law and security procedures.

The parties' agreement that student data is out of scope is a material service-design and contracting condition. Nothing in this Agreement converts TPP into a student records system or authorizes use as one.

## 5. Customer content and rights

"Customer Content" means professional content submitted to TPP by or for Customer, including curriculum, plans, schedules, standards selections, validation records, and related materials.

As between Customer and Brau Consulting, Customer and its users retain whatever rights they otherwise hold in Customer Content. This Agreement does not determine ownership between Customer and its employees.

Customer grants Brau Consulting a limited, non-exclusive right to host, process, transmit, reproduce, format, and otherwise use Customer Content as necessary to provide, secure, support, and maintain TPP; comply with law; and enforce this Agreement.

Customer represents that it has the rights and authority necessary to provide Customer Content to TPP.

## 6. Confidentiality

Each party may receive nonpublic information identified as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential ("Confidential Information"). The receiving party will use Confidential Information only to perform or exercise rights under this Agreement and will protect it using reasonable care, at least the care used for its own similar information.

Confidential Information does not include information that the receiving party can document: was lawfully known without confidentiality obligation; becomes public without breach; is received lawfully from a third party without confidentiality duty; or is independently developed without use of the other party's Confidential Information.

A party may disclose Confidential Information when required by law or valid legal process and, when legally permitted, will provide reasonable advance notice so the other party may seek protection.

## 7. Privacy and security

Brau Consulting will maintain reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the nature of the TPP service and permitted data, as further described in the current Security & Data Practices document.

Brau Consulting will use material subprocessors as identified in the current Subprocessor List and will remain responsible for its contractual obligations concerning the services it provides to Customer.

Customer is responsible for its users, account administration, endpoint/device security, and ensuring that submitted information remains within the agreed data boundary.

If Customer requires a data processing addendum or additional security terms for permitted professional account data, the parties may execute an appropriate addendum. Such an addendum must not imply that student records are an approved TPP data category unless the product and contracting boundary are separately changed through formal governance.

## 8. Security incidents

Brau Consulting will maintain an incident-response process. If Brau Consulting confirms unauthorized acquisition of Customer information requiring notice under applicable law or this Agreement, Brau Consulting will notify Customer without unreasonable delay and provide available information reasonably necessary for Customer's response obligations.

The exact notice period, if a Customer requires a contractually fixed period shorter than otherwise applicable law, must be stated in the order form or negotiated security addendum.

## 9. AI-assisted functionality

TPP may use third-party generative AI services to provide teacher-invoked planning assistance. AI output is a draft and may be inaccurate or incomplete. Customer will ensure users exercise professional review before relying on AI-generated material.

TPP's governed workflow is intended to preserve teacher review and control before AI suggestions alter saved planning content or official exports.

Brau Consulting's production policy is not to opt Customer Content into third-party model training without prior governance review, corresponding disclosure, and any required contractual authorization.

## 10. Standards and external sources

TPP may maintain governed copies of instructional standards and related authoritative source information. Brau Consulting will use reasonable processes to preserve source/version provenance and distinguish authoritative source text from AI-generated interpretation.

Customer acknowledges that standards, guidance, and source publications may change and that AI or software may make errors in mapping or interpretation. Customer remains responsible for final instructional and compliance decisions.

## 11. Acceptable use

Customer and its authorized users will comply with the TPP Acceptable Use Policy. Customer will not use TPP to process prohibited student data, unlawfully obtained information, credentials/secrets, or other information outside the approved service boundary.

## 12. Fees, taxes, and payment

Fees, invoicing, payment schedule, renewal, user quantities, and any implementation charges will be stated in the order form. Unless the order form states otherwise, Customer is responsible for applicable taxes other than taxes based on Brau Consulting's net income.

**Publication blocker:** Standard payment due date, late-payment treatment, renewal model, and pilot-to-paid conversion terms must be approved before commercial use.

## 13. Term and termination

The Agreement begins on the effective date stated in the order form and continues for the stated term. Renewal terms, if any, will be stated in the order form.

Either party may terminate for material breach if the breach remains uncured after reasonable written notice and an opportunity to cure, unless immediate suspension or termination is reasonably required for security, unlawful use, or circumstances that cannot be cured.

Upon expiration or termination, Customer access will end according to the applicable order form and Brau Consulting's retention/deletion procedures. The parties may agree on an export period before deletion.

**Publication blocker:** Default cure period, export window, and post-termination deletion schedule must be approved and technically implemented before final form.
