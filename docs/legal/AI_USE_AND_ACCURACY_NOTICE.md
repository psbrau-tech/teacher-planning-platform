# Teacher Planning Platform — AI Use & Accuracy Notice

**Provider:** Brau Consulting LLC  
**Status:** Pre-Release Draft — Not Yet Effective

Teacher Planning Platform ("TPP") includes teacher-invoked generative artificial intelligence features intended to assist educators with professional planning. This notice explains the role and limitations of those features.

## 1. AI assists; the educator decides

TPP may generate suggestions for learning targets, standards decomposition, Know/Understand/Do components, instructional activities, assessments, resources, literacy or ACT connections where appropriate, weekly reflections, and other planning fields.

AI-generated content is a draft. The educator remains responsible for determining whether a suggestion is accurate, appropriate, lawful, aligned to applicable standards and local policy, pedagogically sound, and suitable for the educator's instructional context.

TPP's governed workflow is intended to preserve teacher control through review, edit, accept, and reject actions. AI-generated material should not silently overwrite saved teacher-approved planning content.

## 2. AI can be wrong

Generative AI may produce inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, inconsistent, biased, misleading, or fabricated information. It may misunderstand curriculum context or infer an alignment that is not supported by the authoritative standard.

Do not rely on an AI response as the sole source for legal requirements, district policy, official standards text, safety requirements, special-education obligations, personnel matters, or other high-impact decisions.

## 3. Authoritative standards are distinct from AI interpretation

TPP is designed to ingest authoritative standards deterministically into governed snapshots with source/version provenance. Official standard text and provenance should remain distinguishable from AI-generated explanations, decompositions, alignments, or instructional suggestions.

An AI suggestion that a lesson aligns to a standard is an instructional planning aid. It does not amend the standard, replace the authoritative source, or establish official compliance.

Where TPP detects a change in an authoritative standards source, the change should be staged through the governed standards process rather than allowing AI to rewrite or silently replace approved source text.

## 4. No student data in AI requests

TPP's service boundary prohibits student personally identifiable information and student education records. Users must not place student data in prompts or other planning fields used for AI assistance.

AI requests should be limited to permitted professional context such as curriculum, standards, schedules, lesson plans, validation information, and generalized instructional needs.

## 5. AI service provider and model-training posture

TPP currently uses OpenAI's API/business services for generative AI features. OpenAI's current Services Agreement states that OpenAI does not use business Customer Content to develop or improve its services unless the customer explicitly agrees to such use.

Brau Consulting's production policy is not to opt TPP customer content into third-party model training without prior governance review, updated disclosures, and any required contractual or user approval.

Provider terms and production account configuration must be reverified before publication and whenever the AI provider or relevant data controls materially change.

## 6. Usage and operational records

TPP may log bounded AI operational metadata, including request status, model identifier, usage measurements, estimated cost, failure information, and educator accept/edit/reject state. These records support reliability, cost control, auditability, and product governance.

TPP must not intentionally place API keys, access tokens, or prohibited student data in AI operational logs.

## 7. Human review expectations

Before using AI-assisted content in an instructional plan or official school document, educators should:
- compare standards references with the authoritative source displayed or cited by TPP;
- verify facts, dates, calculations, citations, and resource references;
- review content for instructional appropriateness and local policy;
- revise language to reflect the educator's actual intent and classroom context;
- reject unsupported or unnecessary suggestions.

## 8. Service changes

AI models and provider capabilities change over time. TPP may change models, prompts, guardrails, or providers when necessary for quality, security, cost, or service reliability, subject to the TPP Privacy Policy, institutional agreements, and governance requirements.

Material changes affecting data use or customer obligations require corresponding privacy/legal review before deployment.
