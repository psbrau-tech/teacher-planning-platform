# Reflection workflow and PLC meeting guide decision

**Decision date:** 2026-08-15  
**Status:** Approved product workflow; implementation governed by existing TPP professional-data boundary

## Teacher Friday closeout

Reflection Intelligence is no longer presented as a floating control throughout teacher planning.

The Friday closeout sequence is:

1. Validate the current week.
2. Complete and submit the teacher-authored Weekly Reflection / PLC Discussion.
3. Review the immutable Completed Weekly Packet.
4. Review private Reflection Insights, if useful.
5. Continue to the following week.

Step 4 is optional. It may synthesize only the teacher's own already-submitted professional reflections. It must not generate, complete, rewrite, or alter the required teacher-authored reflection. It must not block Step 5 when a teacher chooses not to generate a recap.

The private recap remains instructional insight rather than teacher evaluation. It contains no teacher quality score and must not introduce student data.

## School reflection and PLC workflow

The administrator-facing progression is:

**School Reflection Summary → PLC Meeting Guide**

The School Reflection Summary is the governed anonymous aggregate synthesis of submitted teacher-authored professional reflections. It includes the available common successes, common challenges, emerging themes, discussion questions, possible actions, and support needs.

The PLC Meeting Guide must embed the School Reflection Summary so the meeting resource is visibly grounded in what teachers collectively reported rather than operating as a generic agenda. The guide may then add:

- a suggested meeting focus derived from the governed aggregate summary;
- the existing deterministic aggregate formative-assessment planning snapshot when available;
- the fixed facilitation protocol; and
- the non-persistent team action workspace.

Formatting the PLC Meeting Guide does not create a second AI synthesis pass over lesson-plan text. The assessment snapshot remains deterministic. The action workspace remains non-persistent.

## Governance boundary

This decision does not expand TPP's data boundary. No student names, identifiers, grades/results, IEP/504 information, health/discipline information, identifiable student work, or other student-specific information may be introduced.

The School Reflection Summary and PLC Meeting Guide are professional-learning tools. They must not be used for teacher ranking, quality scoring, effort/productivity scoring, personnel evaluation, or student-outcome inference.
